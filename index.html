01 / ABOUT

Who I Am

I am an AI Engineer and ML Researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET).

My work focuses on developing statistically honest and reproducible AI frameworks—integrating causal inference, algorithmic fairness under severe class imbalance, and robust hypothesis testing for mission-critical applications.

Currently, I build autonomous EdTech AI agents and research AI-assisted smart insulin intake technologies at BongoBrain IT, alongside previous experience architecting core banking transactional logic with Temenos T24 and Java.

Beyond engineering, I am a screenwriter and director of 3 independent short films, bringing creative narrative structure and precision to technical systems.
